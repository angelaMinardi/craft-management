# Artist Creative Brief: Pattern Vault Mascot and Illustration System

Use this brief to onboard an illustrator/animator so assets ship quickly and stay consistent with Pattern Vault's existing brand.

## Project Summary

Pattern Vault is an iOS app for organizing craft patterns. The redesign strategy is to increase emotional stickiness and sharing by sequencing moments:

1. Emotion first
2. Value framing
3. Data capture
4. Monetization
5. Sharing

The mascot and supporting illustrations should make this sequence feel warm, reassuring, and rewarding without becoming childish or noisy.

## Creative Objective

Create reusable mascot expressions, poses, and scene elements that make the app feel as endearing and memorable as top-performing mascot-led apps while preserving Pattern Vault's current visual identity.

## Brand Guardrails (Non-Negotiable)

- Keep existing mascot identity and silhouette consistent.
- Use current brand palette only:
  - Warm Cream `#FFF8F0`
  - Soft Coral `#E8836B`
  - Deep Plum `#4A2040`
  - Sage Green `#A8C5A0`
  - Dusty Blue `#8FAABD`
  - Honey `#E8B84B`
- Tone: cozy, capable, encouraging, craft-forward.
- Avoid visual directions that feel neon, harsh, or "high-tech startup."
- Do not introduce a new mascot species or major redesign of the character.

## Target Emotions by Product Moment

- Welcome/onboarding: curious, friendly, inviting
- First win (save/import): proud, excited, encouraging
- Progress updates: focused, supportive, "you got this"
- Celebration/completion: joyful, energetic, share-worthy
- Errors/recovery: empathetic, calm, reassuring

## Deliverable Formats

- Source: layered vector files (AI/Figma/SVG)
- Optional source: layered PSD for raster workflows
- Exports: transparent PNG at @1x, @2x, @3x where relevant
- Animation-ready: separated body parts/layers when possible

## Technical Constraints

- Assets must remain lightweight for iOS and share extension usage.
- Maintain clean transparent edges (no matte fringe).
- Ensure readability at small sizes (mobile-first).
- Keep consistent naming and versioning.

## Do / Do Not

### Do

- Prioritize reusable modular assets over one-off illustrations.
- Keep expressions readable within one second.
- Leave text-safe zones for UI overlays.
- Preserve personality consistency across expressions.

### Do Not

- Over-detail scenes with cluttered backgrounds.
- Use thin strokes that disappear on small screens.
- Convey critical meaning with color alone.
- Add heavy animation concepts that require new runtime systems.

## Production Schedule (Recommended)

- Week 1: style frames + expression exploration
- Weeks 2-3: core mascot kit + onboarding scenes
- Week 4: celebration/recovery/web pack
- Week 5: implementation QA revision round

## Approval Criteria

An asset is approved only if it passes all checks:

- Emotional clarity is immediate
- Brand fidelity is intact
- UI compatibility on small screens
- Correct formats and naming
- Reusable across multiple moments
