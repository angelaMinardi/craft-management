# Privacy Policy

Corvid Craft ("Application") is operated by Angela Minardi ("Service Provider") as a freemium mobile service.

## Information Collection and Use

The Application collects and processes information needed to provide core product functionality. This may include:

* Account information (for example, email address and account identifier)
* Pattern/library content you save (for example, URLs, titles, descriptions, tags, notes, and linked metadata)
* User content you create (for example, project notes, stash/tool inventory details, and optional attached photos)
* Service and usage metadata (for example, subscription/entitlement status and feature usage counters such as monthly AI usage)
* Device/app technical data needed for operation and security

The Application does not request or collect precise device location data.

The Application uses AI features (currently Gemini-based flows) for import analysis, step parsing, and optional yarn-label photo scanning. Content you submit for those features — including yarn-label photos you choose to scan — may be processed by AI providers to return structured results.

The Service Provider may use your contact information for required account, legal, and service communications.

## Third-Party Services

The Application relies on the following third-party services and sub-processors. Some are optional and depend on runtime/build configuration.

| Sub-processor | Purpose | Data involved |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Supabase](https://supabase.com/privacy) | Hosting, database, authentication, file storage | Account, pattern/library content, user content, usage metadata |
| [Google Gemini API](https://ai.google.dev/terms) | AI import analysis, step parsing, and yarn-label scanning | Pattern content and yarn-label photos you submit for those features |
| [Google AdMob](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543?hl=en) | Free-tier advertising | Device/advertising identifiers (only with your ATT consent) |
| [Google Analytics for Firebase](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy) | Usage analytics (optional) | App-event and device data |
| [Firebase Crashlytics](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy/) | Crash diagnostics (optional) | Crash and device data |
| [Ravelry](https://www.ravelry.com/about/privacy) | Optional account connect, pattern search/import | Only if you connect Ravelry: your Ravelry authorization and the patterns you choose to import |
| [Vercel](https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy) | Hosting for our website and the web pattern parser | Request metadata; parser input is processed in memory and not stored |
| [UPCitemdb](https://www.upcitemdb.com/) | Optional barcode lookup to auto-fill yarn stash details | The scanned barcode number only, when you scan a yarn barcode |

Notes:
* AdMob is used for free-tier ads and is gated behind your App Tracking Transparency choice; declining does not change app functionality.
* Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics are optional and only active when Firebase is configured for the app build.
* Ravelry is only contacted if you choose to connect your Ravelry account or use pattern search.
* Barcode lookups send only the scanned barcode number to UPCitemdb — never your account or device details.
* The Service Provider does not sell personal data.

## Data Sharing and Disclosure

The Service Provider may disclose information:

* as required by law or legal process;
* to protect rights, safety, or investigate fraud/security issues;
* to service providers acting on the Service Provider's behalf under applicable contractual/privacy obligations.

## Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live (including under the EU/UK GDPR and the California CCPA/CPRA), you have rights over your personal data, which may include the right to:

* **Access** the personal data we hold about you;
* **Correct** inaccurate or incomplete data;
* **Delete** your account and associated data;
* **Export / port** a copy of data you provided;
* **Object to or restrict** certain processing, and **withdraw consent** (for example, by declining App Tracking Transparency or disconnecting Ravelry);
* **Not be discriminated against** for exercising these rights.

To exercise any of these rights, contact the Service Provider at support@corvidcraft.com. We do not sell personal data and do not use it for cross-context behavioral advertising beyond the AdMob consent described above.

## Deleting Your Account and Data

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time, directly in the app: **Settings → Delete Account**. This permanently removes your account and your patterns, notes, stash/tools, and uploaded files. You can also stop further collection by uninstalling the Application, or request deletion by emailing support@corvidcraft.com.

## Data Retention Policy

The Service Provider retains data for as long as needed to provide the service and meet legal/operational obligations. When you delete your account, associated data is removed from the production database; backups and third-party logs are purged on their normal rotation cycles.

## Children

The Application is not directed to children under 13. The Service Provider does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact support@corvidcraft.com.

## Security

The Service Provider uses reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect personal information.
